# Why Firefox?

Firefox is one of the last truly independent web browsers standing in the way of Google's Chromium monopoly. The browser is also free, open source, & highly customizable. **There is simply no other browser out there that allows for the level of configuration that Phoenix provides.**

**Mozilla is far from perfect, but we believe Firefox is still the best browser on the market in terms of privacy & user freedom.**

# Why not just fork Firefox and create your own browser like LibreWolf?

The largest reason is security updates. I am the only person working on this project, and I am not currently in a position to guarantee
consistent and timely browser security updates for a desktop browser like this. Things happen.
[The majority of browser forks are simply hobbyist projects that do not take matters like this seriously](https://web.archive.org/web/20231214153202/https://github.com/Alex313031/thorium/issues/463),
but Phoenix is different: We put privacy and security at the forefront of everything we do. I am not willing to put my users at risk, now or ever.

**The fact of the matter is that due to Firefox on Desktop's strong support for customization and advanced configuration, there's not a lot that a browser fork can offer over just sticking to it as a base.**
